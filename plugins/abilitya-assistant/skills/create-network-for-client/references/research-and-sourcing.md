## Source hierarchy

Use current web research. Prefer sources in this order:

1. the client's official website, newsroom, store, ticketing, booking, league profile, and verified social accounts;
2. governing bodies, competition organizers, event organizers, filings, or other primary sources;
3. reputable major news outlets and specialist publications with named reporting;
4. LinkedIn, Reddit, and community sources for sentiment or topic discovery, never as sole authority for a factual claim that requires verification.

Compare publication date with event date. Avoid stale pages presented as current news.

## Client identity and links

Resolve:

- official display name and category;
- official domain and locale-appropriate destination;
- official logo or a user-supplied logo;
- verified social profiles;
- authoritative store, booking, and ticket URLs when relevant;
- audience, products, teams, competitions, locations, and editorial themes for realistic interests.

Do not use search-result redirect URLs, tracking-heavy mirrors, fan logos, counterfeit stores, ticket resellers, or unverified domains when an official destination exists. Remove unnecessary tracking parameters when the canonical URL is clear.

## Brand and theme inputs

For football clients, find current official home and away kits. Use the darker/home kit to inspire dark mode and the white/lighter kit for light mode when those roles fit. Extract a primary accent, supporting accent, and neutral direction without claiming exact official color codes unless a brand guide verifies them.

For other clients, prefer official brand guidelines, website UI, product packaging, campaign art, or supplied references. Ensure the chosen palettes remain accessible after translating them into the complete live theme.

## Social video

Target the requested Story count with recent client-owned vertical videos:

- search verified Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels/video, YouTube Shorts, and other official feeds;
- prioritize varied subjects instead of seven near-identical clips;
- download only when the public source and available tooling permit it;
- preserve the original social URL and platform attribution;
- upload actual video media and attach it to `story.medias`;
- never substitute a still image for a required Story video.

If Instagram blocks automated public media access, continue with official Facebook or YouTube Shorts. Report the fallback without treating it as a blocker.

## Editorial sources and covers

Opinion Posts should synthesize multiple sources and present a clear point of view. Each Post must include at least two relevant source URLs in `links`, plus a cover from an official or properly attributable source.

Link/Web contents should use different timely topics. Extract or upload a valid image and pass it as the cover even when the live schema makes it optional. Do not create text-only Web cards for this content workflow.

For Announcements, research client assets first, verified sponsor or partner assets second, and official generic-advertiser campaigns third. Generic ads must not imply a client relationship. Follow the Content capability's **Announcement assets** workflow for preparation and verification.

Verified Events need a relevant cover and source URL in the description or accepted link field. Original network-owned community Events should use client-owned evergreen imagery, realistic audience-facing copy, and only claims controlled by the network; do not invent third-party involvement or add disclaimers.

## Rights and attribution

Prefer client-owned assets because the network represents the client's identity. Keep source URLs in the internal completion report and in Content fields where the experience supports attribution. Do not imply endorsement, exclusivity, confirmation, or ownership beyond what the source establishes.
